## Context

What is the issue that we're seeing that is motivating this decision? Describe the forces at play, including
technical, business, and team constraints.

## Decision

State the decision clearly and concisely, in active voice: "We will ...".

## Alternatives considered

What other options were on the table, and why were they not chosen? (Optional — omit if there was only one
reasonable option.)

## Consequences

What becomes easier or more difficult to do because of this change? Include trade-offs and any follow-up work
this creates.
